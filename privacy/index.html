Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026 · Applies to: backbonegrowth.com · This policy complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Who we are

Backbone Services Group provides social media management, property management, and lead generation services to American small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, request an audit, or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Audit requests: when you request a free audit, we collect the details in the form — business name, your name, email address, phone number, business type, service area, and the service-specific details you provide (such as property address and monthly rent for a profitability analysis, or social profiles for a social media audit).
  • Communications: if you email us or contact us directly, we keep the content of that communication.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Basic analytics: pages visited, approximate location, device type, referring site, and timestamps (aggregated, not sold).

3. How we use your information

  • To deliver the audit, analysis, or service you requested.
  • To communicate with you about your request and our services.
  • To send occasional marketing emails about Backbone Services Group — only if you agreed, and you can opt out anytime with one click.
  • To improve our website and services.

We never sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Legal bases & retention

We process your information to fulfill your request (contract), with your consent (marketing), and for legitimate business interests (fraud prevention, analytics). We keep personal information only as long as needed for these purposes, and we delete audit-request data within 12 months unless you become a client.

5. Your rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and share.
  • Access a copy of your personal information (twice per 12-month period, free).
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" — we do not sell or share your data, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Non-discrimination: we will never treat you differently for exercising these rights.

6. How to exercise your rights

Email hello@backbonegrowth.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We verify your identity before acting, and we respond within 45 days (extendable by another 45 days with notice). You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

7. Cookies & third-party tools

We use minimal cookies for analytics. Our audit forms are powered by Kit (ConvertKit), which processes form submissions under its own terms; Kit does not sell your data. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

8. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the revised policy.
